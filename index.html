04 / THE PRINCIPALAN OPERATOR’S PERSPECTIVE

ONE PRINCIPAL.
MANY DIMENSIONS.

I’m Josephine Petra Lovensa.Founder. Commercial leader. Hands-on builder.

Most define full-stack as front-end to back-end. At JOPELO, it means codebases to concrete: bridging digital product engineering and physical real estate development.

My experience spans business leadership at Fjall Group, founding SECHA, and shaping strategy at LingoTalk. Along the way: recognition from Forbes India, APAC Insider, and The Women’s Tabloid Award, venture-building through Antler, and a background with UNHCR. Different contexts, one consistent approach: understand the problem, build the foundation, and follow through.
